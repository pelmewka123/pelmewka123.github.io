Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Dota Panel ("the app") is a personal desktop tool that runs only on its owner's own computer.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube: with your permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos you select to your own channel and to check their upload status.
  • TikTok: with your permission, the app sends videos you select to your own TikTok drafts (inbox) and reads basic account info needed to do so.

How data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Videos, titles and descriptions stay on your computer until you choose to upload them.

Sharing

The app has no server of its own. It does not collect, sell or share your data with anyone. Data goes only to YouTube or TikTok, and only when you press "Publish".

Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke access at any time in your Google Account (Security → Third-party apps) or in TikTok settings, and by deleting the app's local data.
